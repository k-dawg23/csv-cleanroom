CSV Cleanroom

Preview and clean a CSV on your computer. Your browser sends the file to this local application, not an external service.

Spreadsheet formula warning

CSV values and headings beginning with formula-like characters may be interpreted as formulas by spreadsheet applications. CSV quoting does not make them safe. CSV Cleanroom does not automatically neutralise formulas. For untrusted data, use your spreadsheet application's text-import workflow and explicitly import columns as text; do not simply double-click the CSV.
